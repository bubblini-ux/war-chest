# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Work on `war-chest` deals with sensitive historical artifacts from World War II, strict firearms regulations, and high-trust client and consignor relationships. Everyone working on this project is expected to uphold the following standards:

1. **Historical Stewardship and Respect:** Treat historical artifacts with scholarly, archival seriousness. Never celebrate, romanticize, or glorify war, violence, atrocities, or the Nazi regime (see [`Compliance/Listing Rules.md`](./Compliance/Listing%20Rules.md) and [`Marketing/Brand and Voice.md`](./Marketing/Brand%20and%20Voice.md)).
2. **Strict Legal Compliance:** Never bypass, downplay, or help anyone circumvent federal, state, local, or international firearms laws, transfer paperwork, or background checks. Immediately escalate any red flags listed in [`Compliance/Compliance Overview.md`](./Compliance/Compliance%20Overview.md) and [`Operations/Sale Workflow.md`](./Operations/Sale%20Workflow.md).
3. **Confidentiality and Privacy:** Respect the privacy and physical security of the client, consignors, veterans' families, and buyers in accordance with [`Compliance/Confidentiality Standard.md`](./Compliance/Confidentiality%20Standard.md).
4. **Honesty and Precision:** Represent provenance, condition, and legal status accurately. Never fabricate or exaggerate historical claims.
5. **Professional Collaboration:** Communicate respectfully, constructively, and plainly with team members, contractors, legal counsel, and the client.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Publishing or committing **Restricted** information (full serial numbers, storage locations, personal identity documents, licence scans, or credentials).
- Publishing instructions for reactivating, converting, or illegally modifying weapons.
- Using violent, sensational, or extremist imagery or language in project materials, code, or communications.
- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks against any collaborator, client, or community member.
- Concealing a suspected legal violation, compliance red flag, or data leak.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report any violation of this Code of Conduct, compliance red flag, or security concern immediately to the project lead. Nothing in this policy or any confidentiality agreement restricts reporting a suspected violation of law to a government authority (see [`Compliance/Confidentiality Standard.md`](./Compliance/Confidentiality%20Standard.md)).
