# Discovery Questions

Back to [[Home]]. Record the answers in [[Client Profile]].

## The business

1. How long have you been selling, and how do you sell today?
2. Where is the business based, and where are your buyers?
3. Who else works in the business?
4. Which of our sites did you see, and what stood out?

## Licences and legal

1. What federal and state licences do you hold?
2. Do you handle machine guns or other items regulated under the National Firearms Act?
3. Who is your firearms attorney?
4. Do you sell abroad or import anything?

## The stock

1. What do you sell: firearms, edged weapons, deactivated pieces, accessories, or militaria?
2. How many items are for sale at any one time, and how fast do they turn over?
3. What paperwork and photographs do you already have for each item?
4. Do you take consignments or run auctions?
5. Do any items carry Nazi markings, and how do you want those presented?

## The website

1. Should buyers purchase online, or send an enquiry first?
2. Should prices be public or available on request?
3. Who will add new items after launch?
4. Do you have a payment processor that accepts your business?
5. Which sites in this field do you admire or dislike?

## The project

1. What does success look like a year from now?
2. What are your budget and deadline?
3. Who approves the work?
