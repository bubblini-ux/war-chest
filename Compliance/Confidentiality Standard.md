# Confidentiality Standard

Back to [[Home]]. Every piece of project information falls into one of four levels. If unsure, use the higher level.

## Levels

1. **Public:** approved listings, published articles, and the live website. Can go anywhere.
2. **Internal:** our plans, drafts, and notes without customer secrets. Stays in this vault and in project tools.
3. **Confidential:** customer identity before announcement, unpublished inventory, costs, consignors, buyers, and quotes. Shared only with people covered by a signed agreement.
4. **Restricted:** full serial numbers, firearm transfer records, registration papers, licence copies, identity documents, passwords, keys, storage locations, and security details. Stays in the customer's own secure records.

## Rules for this vault

1. Never put Restricted information in this vault.
2. Never put this vault in a public repository. If it goes into Git, confirm the repository is private first.
3. Keep passwords and access keys out of notes and use a password manager.
4. Use stock numbers in notes instead of serial numbers.
5. Keep client material out of AI tools and third-party services unless the customer has agreed to their use.
6. Keep public item photos free of full serial numbers, storage areas, and security equipment.

## Which agreement to use

1. Before the customer shares anything sensitive: [[Mutual NDA Template]]
2. Before a contractor sees client material: [[Contractor Confidentiality Template]]

## Limits of confidentiality

Confidentiality never covers hiding illegal activity. Agreements must allow required firearm records, lawful inspections, and reporting suspected crimes. See [[Compliance Overview]].

## If something leaks

1. Stop further exposure and revoke any affected access.
2. Write down what was exposed, when, and who it affects.
3. Tell the customer promptly, as the agreement requires.
4. Ask an attorney whether a breach notification is legally required.
