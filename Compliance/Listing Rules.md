# Listing Rules

Back to [[Home]]. These apply to every item on the site, alongside [[Compliance Overview]].

## Every listing must

1. State plainly whether the item is a live firearm, deactivated, a replica, or a non-firing artifact.
2. State any legal requirement for buying it, including transfer through a licensed dealer.
3. Say where the item can't be shipped, or link to a clear restrictions page.
4. Describe condition honestly, including refinishing, replaced parts, and mismatched numbers.
5. Claim only the history that the documents support. See [[Provenance and Authentication]].

## Never list

- Any unregistered machine gun or other unregistered National Firearms Act item
- Live ordnance
- Anything with a removed or altered serial number
- Anything the customer can't legally transfer

## Never publish

- Instructions for reactivating, converting, or modifying a weapon
- Full serial numbers on public pages, since these make forged paperwork easier. Keep them in private records and show only part of the number if buyers need it.
- Personal details of previous owners or veterans' families without permission

## Nazi markings and memorabilia

Many genuine items carry these markings, so we need a clear policy.

1. Keep the tone historical. Never celebrate or romanticize the regime.
2. Use neutral terms in descriptions and avoid using the symbols as decoration in site design, ads, or social images.
3. Some countries, including Germany and France, restrict display or sale of these symbols. Consider this when serving or shipping abroad.
4. Payment and social platforms may have their own rules, as noted in [[Platform Restrictions]].
5. Agree the wording and image policy with the customer in writing.
