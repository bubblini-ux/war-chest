# Platform Restrictions

Back to [[Home]]. Providers change these policies often, so read each provider's current terms and get written confirmation before committing. A sudden account ban could take down the site or freeze funds.

## Check each of these

- **Payment processors:** mainstream processors such as Stripe, PayPal, and Square generally prohibit firearm sales. Expect to need a firearms-friendly merchant account.
- **Store platforms:** some allow firearms only with conditions, and their built-in payments may not be available.
- **Hosting:** confirm the host's acceptable use policy covers firearm sales. Avoid hidden dependence on a single provider.
- **Advertising:** Google and Meta generally prohibit ads promoting weapons. Plan as though paid ads on these platforms are unavailable.
- **Email services:** some ban firearm promotion. Check before building a mailing list.
- **Social media:** posts may be limited even when ads are banned. Accounts can be restricted without warning.
- **Marketplaces:** specialist firearm auction sites exist, and general marketplaces such as eBay ban firearms.

## Our rules

1. Get acceptance in writing for payments, hosting, and email.
2. Keep regular backups of the site and customer list outside the host.
3. Never disguise what the business sells to get approved. It breaches the terms, and funds can be frozen when it's discovered.
