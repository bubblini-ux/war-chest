# Cataloging Standard

Back to [[Home]]. Every item gets one note made from [[Item Template]]. Check public wording against [[Listing Rules]].

## Public details

1. Item name and model
2. Nation of origin and service branch
3. Maker and year of manufacture, if known
4. Category from [[Item Categories]]
5. Legal status in plain words
6. Condition, including any restoration
7. History the documents support
8. Price, or "on request"
9. Availability and shipping limits

## Private details

These must stay out of the public site.

1. Full serial number
2. Acquisition source and cost
3. Consignor details
4. Scans of sensitive documents
5. Registration paperwork

The customer's licensed records remain the official record. This vault doesn't replace them. Consider keeping sensitive details only in the customer's own secure system.

## Photography

1. Take full views of both sides, then close-ups of markings, wear, and any flaws.
2. Use consistent lighting and a plain background.
3. Hide or blur full serial numbers on public images.
4. Write alt text for every image describing what matters to a collector. See [[Accessibility Standard]].

## File names

Use the form: year, nation, model, and stock number. For example: `1943 US M1 Garand A1042`.
