# Item Categories

Back to [[Home]]. Adjust once the customer confirms what they sell. Legal terms are explained in [[Compliance Overview]].

## Standard firearms

Service rifles, pistols, and revolvers. Most can be sold through the normal licensed dealer transfer. State restrictions still apply.

## National Firearms Act items

Registered machine guns, submachine guns, and similar items. These need a site flow based on enquiry, never instant checkout, and the customer needs the right licence status.

## Deactivated pieces

Registration status must be confirmed for each piece. The listing has to say exactly what kind of deactivation it is.

## Edged weapons

Bayonets, fighting knives, swords, and daggers. Often simpler to sell, but state and platform rules can apply.

## Accessories and field gear

Holsters, slings, cleaning kits, helmets, and web gear. Some magazines may be restricted by state.

## Documents and memorabilia

Capture papers, photographs, unit records, and medals. Laws may limit sales of some US military decorations, so check before listing any.

## Inert ordnance

Only with documented proof it is inert. If there is any doubt, follow the live ordnance rule in [[Compliance Overview]].
