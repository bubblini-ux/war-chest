# Brand and Voice

Back to [[Home]]. Position the customer as a careful steward of history. Confirm this direction with them.

## Voice

1. Knowledgeable, plain, and calm
2. Precise about facts and honest about unknowns
3. Respectful of the people who served and those who suffered

## Words to avoid

1. Macho or violent marketing language
2. Hype such as "insane" or "killer deal"
3. Any glorification of war, atrocities, or the Nazi regime
4. Unsupported words such as "battle-proven" or "museum quality"

## Example description

"This rifle was made in 1943 and retains its original finish, with wear consistent with service use. Papers from the veteran's family are included. Sold as a firearm and must transfer through a licensed dealer."

## Look and feel

To be designed after the customer call. Think archive or fine auction house, with room for photographs. Avoid camouflage, bullet holes, and military stencil clichés. Follow the symbol rules in [[Listing Rules]].
