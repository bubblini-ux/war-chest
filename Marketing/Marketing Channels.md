# Marketing Channels

Back to [[Home]]. Paid ads on the big platforms are likely unavailable, so earned reach matters most. See [[Platform Restrictions]].

## Priority order

1. **Search:** item pages, sold archives, and reference articles that answer what collectors look for
2. **Email list:** new arrivals, after confirming the email provider permits it
3. **Collector communities:** forums and associations for specific makers and nations, following their rules on selling
4. **Gun shows and militaria shows:** collect signups and show the catalog
5. **Specialist auction and listing sites:** use them for reach and link back when allowed
6. **Referrals:** estate attorneys, appraisers, museums, and veterans' organizations, for people looking to sell a collection
7. **Video and social:** share history and restoration stories, and expect limits

## Measuring results

Keep it simple:

1. Qualified enquiries per month
2. Consignment leads
3. Email signups
4. Sales that began on the site
