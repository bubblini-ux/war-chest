# Sale Workflow

Back to [[Home]]. A draft process for the site to support. The customer's attorney confirms the details against [[Compliance Overview]].

## Standard firearm

1. The buyer sends an enquiry or reserves the item.
2. The site shows state restrictions before the buyer commits.
3. The customer confirms the item can legally go to that state.
4. The buyer names a licensed dealer near them.
5. The customer verifies that dealer's licence.
6. The buyer pays through the approved processor.
7. The item ships to the dealer with insurance and signature required.
8. The dealer runs the background check and completes the transfer.
9. The customer records the sale in their licensed records.

## National Firearms Act item

1. Enquiry only, with no online checkout.
2. The customer confirms the registration and the buyer's eligibility.
3. The transfer goes through the required ATF forms and approval.
4. The item stays with the customer until approval arrives.
5. Tell buyers up front that approval can take time.

## Curio and relic licence holder

Only if the customer's attorney approves direct shipment. Verify a signed copy of the buyer's licence first.

## Consignment

1. Use a written agreement covering commission, insurance, and unsold items.
2. The customer takes the item into their licensed records as required.
3. Complete [[Provenance Record Template]] before listing.

## Warning signs

Pause the sale and ask the customer to review it if the buyer:

1. Asks to skip the transfer process
2. Offers extra payment for no paperwork
3. Appears to be buying for someone else
4. Wants delivery somewhere that doesn't fit their stated location
