# Roadmap

This roadmap mirrors the operational checklist in [`Client/Project Tracker.md`](./Client/Project%20Tracker.md) and tracks progress from initial client onboarding through post-launch growth.

## Phase 1: Legal Onboarding & Discovery (Current)

- [ ] Have legal counsel review [`Templates/Mutual NDA Template.md`](./Templates/Mutual%20NDA%20Template.md) and execute it before receiving sensitive client information.
- [ ] Conduct the discovery call using [`Client/Discovery Questions.md`](./Client/Discovery%20Questions.md).
- [ ] Populate [`Client/Client Profile.md`](./Client/Client%20Profile.md) with confirmed jurisdiction, licence types, inventory scope, budget, and timeline.
- [ ] Verify the client's licences and jurisdiction against [`Compliance/Compliance Overview.md`](./Compliance/Compliance%20Overview.md).
- [ ] Have the client's firearms attorney review [`Compliance/Listing Rules.md`](./Compliance/Listing%20Rules.md) and [`Operations/Sale Workflow.md`](./Operations/Sale%20Workflow.md).

## Phase 2: Vendor Verification & Scoping

- [ ] Obtain written acceptable-use confirmation from hosting, form/email, and payment providers per [`Compliance/Platform Restrictions.md`](./Compliance/Platform%20Restrictions.md).
- [ ] Confirm enquiry-only vs. invoice/merchant checkout flow with the client.
- [ ] Estimate initial catalog volume and photography/documentation effort using [`Inventory/Cataloging Standard.md`](./Inventory/Cataloging%20Standard.md).
- [ ] Deliver project quote and statement of work.

## Phase 3: Design, Architecture & Build

- [ ] Finalize page structure and navigation from [`Website/Site Plan.md`](./Website/Site%20Plan.md).
- [ ] Select and configure the static site generator, CMS/editor, and enquiry handler per [`Website/Tech and Payments.md`](./Website/Tech%20and%20Payments.md).
- [ ] Establish visual identity and editorial guidelines with the client using [`Marketing/Brand and Voice.md`](./Marketing/Brand%20and%20Voice.md).
- [ ] Build templates and collection filters meeting [`Website/Accessibility Standard.md`](./Website/Accessibility%20Standard.md) (WCAG 2.2 AA).
- [ ] Catalog the initial launch collection using [`Templates/Item Template.md`](./Templates/Item%20Template.md) and [`Templates/Provenance Record Template.md`](./Templates/Provenance%20Record%20Template.md).

## Phase 4: Pre-Launch Review & Launch

- [ ] Complete automated and manual keyboard/screen-reader accessibility audits.
- [ ] Verify all item images have full serial numbers hidden and EXIF/location metadata stripped.
- [ ] Conduct final legal review of live listings, disclaimers, and state restriction notices with the client's firearms attorney.
- [ ] Launch the site on the client-owned domain and configure automated off-host backups.

## Phase 5: Post-Launch & Organic Growth

- [ ] Execute priority outreach channels in [`Marketing/Marketing Channels.md`](./Marketing/Marketing%20Channels.md).
- [ ] Publish initial collector guides and historical articles from [`Marketing/Content Ideas.md`](./Marketing/Content%20Ideas.md).
- [ ] Transition sold listings into the permanent historical reference archive to build search authority.
