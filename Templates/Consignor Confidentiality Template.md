# Consignor Confidentiality and Publicity Agreement

Template note: use alongside the consignment contract described in [[Sale Workflow]], and record the publicity choices in the [[Provenance Record Template]]. Replace everything in square brackets, delete this line, and have an attorney review the agreement before anyone signs it.

This Agreement is made on [date] between [customer legal business name] (the "Dealer") and [consignor name] (the "Consignor").

## 1. What the Dealer Will Keep Confidential

The Dealer will keep the following confidential:

1. The Consignor's identity and contact details
2. The location of the Consignor's home, collection, and storage
3. The value of the Consignor's collection, except for sale prices the Consignor approves for publication
4. Family and estate information shared in connection with the consignment

## 2. Publicity Choices

The Consignor chooses the following. Anything left unchecked is not permitted.

- [ ] The item's history may be published without naming anyone
- [ ] The veteran's name may be published: [name as it should appear]
- [ ] The veteran's unit and service details may be published
- [ ] Family photographs and documents may be published, as listed here: [list]
- [ ] The Consignor's name may be published
- [ ] The sale price may be published after the sale

The Dealer will not publish the Consignor's address or the location of any collection under any choice above. The Consignor may withdraw a choice in writing. The Dealer will then remove the material from its website within [10] business days, but cannot recall material already printed or copied by others.

## 3. Buyers

The Dealer may give a buyer the documents and history that come with the item, limited by the choices above. Where possible, other personal details will be removed from those documents.

## 4. Permitted and Required Disclosures

The Dealer may share information:

1. As required for licensed firearm records, regulated transfers, and tax records
2. With a government agency or court as required by law, including during a lawful inspection
3. With insurers, shippers, attorneys, and appraisers who need it for the consignment and must keep it confidential

Nothing in this Agreement limits reporting a suspected crime or legal violation, and it does not protect any item from legally required handling or reporting.

## 5. The Consignor's Confirmation

The Consignor confirms that they have the right to consign the item, and that the information they provide about its ownership and history is true to the best of their knowledge.

## 6. Term and General

These duties continue for [5] years after the last sale under the consignment. Duties covering the Consignor's address, location of any collection, and personal information continue as long as the Dealer holds that information. This Agreement is governed by the laws of [state or country] and can be changed only in a signed writing.
