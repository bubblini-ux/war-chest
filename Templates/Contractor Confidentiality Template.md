# Contractor Confidentiality Agreement

This Agreement is made on [date] between bubblini-ux (the "Company") and [contractor legal name] (the "Contractor").

## 1. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means non-public information the Contractor receives or creates while working for the Company, including client identities, inventory, pricing, buyers, consignors, storage or security details, credentials, source code, and working methods.

It does not include information that is public through no fault of the Contractor, or that the Contractor lawfully knew or independently developed without using Confidential Information.

## 2. Contractor's Duties

The Contractor will:

1. Use Confidential Information only to perform work for the Company
2. Share it only with people the Company approves in writing
3. Store it only in systems the Company approves
4. Not paste Confidential Information into outside services, including artificial intelligence tools, without the Company's written approval
5. Not post client work or name a client in a portfolio or on social media without written approval
6. Protect passwords and access keys, and report suspected exposure immediately

## 3. Item Access and Images

Unless the work requires it and the Company approves in writing, the Contractor will not record, publish, or share full serial numbers, the location of any collection, or photographs that reveal a storage area or security equipment.

## 4. Protected Rights

Nothing in this Agreement limits the Contractor from reporting a suspected violation of law to a government agency, cooperating with a lawful investigation, or making disclosures protected by law.

Under 18 U.S.C. section 1833(b), an individual will not be held criminally or civilly liable under federal or state trade secret law for disclosing a trade secret (a) in confidence to a federal, state, or local government official or to an attorney, solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law, or (b) in a complaint or other document filed under seal in a lawsuit or other proceeding. An individual who files a lawsuit for retaliation for reporting a suspected violation of law may disclose the trade secret to their attorney and use it in the court proceeding, if any document containing the trade secret is filed under seal and the trade secret is not disclosed except under court order.

## 5. End of Work

When the work ends or on request, the Contractor will return or securely delete Confidential Information and remove any access it holds, then confirm in writing that this is done.

## 6. Term

These duties continue for [5] years after the work ends. They continue for trade secrets as long as the information remains a trade secret, and for personal information as long as the Contractor holds it.

## 7. General

This Agreement is governed by the laws of [state or country]. The Company may seek injunctive relief for a breach. This Agreement does not create employment or guarantee work. It can be changed only in a signed writing.
