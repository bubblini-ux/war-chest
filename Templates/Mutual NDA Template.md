# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Agreement is made on [date] between bubblini-ux, of [address], and [customer legal business name], of [address]. Each is a "Party."

## 1. Purpose

The Parties may share information to evaluate and carry out website, marketing, and related services for the customer's collectible firearms and militaria business (the "Purpose").

## 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means non-public information one Party shares with the other for the Purpose, in any form, if it is marked confidential or a reasonable person would understand it to be confidential. It includes:

1. Inventory, acquisition sources, costs, pricing, and sales records
2. Customer, buyer, and consignor identities and contact details
3. Storage locations and security arrangements
4. Licence and regulatory documents
5. Business plans, quotes, designs, source code, and working methods

## 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that the receiving Party can show:

1. Is or becomes public through no breach of this Agreement
2. It already lawfully knew without a duty of confidentiality
3. It lawfully received from a third party free to share it
4. It developed independently without using the other Party's Confidential Information

Personal information remains protected as required by law even if an exclusion applies.

## 4. Obligations

The receiving Party will:

1. Use Confidential Information only for the Purpose
2. Protect it with at least reasonable care
3. Share it only with employees, contractors, and advisers who need it for the Purpose and are bound by confidentiality duties at least as protective as these
4. Promptly tell the other Party about any known unauthorized access or disclosure

## 5. Sensitive Records

The customer will not send the following unless the Parties agree in writing that it is necessary: full serial numbers, firearm transfer records, registration documents, identity documents, or storage and security details.

## 6. Permitted and Required Disclosures

1. A Party may disclose Confidential Information when required by law, court order, or a government agency. Where lawful, it will give prompt notice and disclose only what is required.
2. Nothing in this Agreement limits required record keeping, a lawful regulatory inspection, cooperation with law enforcement, or reporting a suspected crime or legal violation to a government authority.
3. This Agreement does not require either Party to conceal unlawful conduct.

## 7. Ownership and No Commitment

Each Party keeps ownership of its Confidential Information. This Agreement grants no licence and does not require either Party to enter into further business. Except as stated in another signed agreement, Confidential Information is provided as is.

## 8. Return or Destruction

Within [30] days of written request, the receiving Party will return or destroy Confidential Information. It may keep copies required by law or held in routine backups, and those copies remain protected by this Agreement.

## 9. Term

This Agreement covers disclosures made for [2] years from its date. The duties in it last [5] years after the last disclosure. They continue for trade secrets as long as the information remains a trade secret, and for personal information as long as the receiving Party holds it.

## 10. Remedies

Unauthorized use or disclosure may cause harm that money cannot fully repair, so a Party may seek injunctive relief in addition to other available remedies.

## 11. General

This Agreement is governed by the laws of [state or country], with disputes heard in [courts or venue]. It is the Parties' entire agreement on this subject and can be changed only in a signed writing. Neither Party may assign it without the other's written consent, except to a successor of its business. If part of it is unenforceable, the rest remains in effect.
