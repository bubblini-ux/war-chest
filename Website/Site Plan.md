# Site Plan

Back to [[Home]]. Confirm against the answers in [[Discovery Questions]]. We recommend starting with enquiries instead of checkout. It's simpler, suits rare high-value items, and reduces the compliance and payment risk.

## Pages

1. **Home:** featured pieces and the customer's credibility
2. **Collection:** browse and filter, using [[Item Categories]]
3. **Item page:** photos, history, legal status, and enquiry button
4. **Recent sales:** archive of sold items, which builds credibility and search traffic
5. **Journal:** articles from [[Content Ideas]]
6. **How to buy:** the transfer process in plain words, from [[Sale Workflow]]
7. **Legal and shipping:** state restrictions and policies
8. **Sell or consign:** form for collectors and families
9. **About:** the customer's experience and standards
10. **Contact**

## Features

1. Filter and search that work by keyboard and screen reader
2. Clear legal status on every item
3. Enquiry form that records the item and buyer's state
4. Saved items and alerts for new arrivals, once email is approved
5. An easy admin screen, so the customer can add items

## Deliberately left out at launch

1. Instant checkout
2. Buyer accounts, unless the customer needs them
3. Ads for third parties
